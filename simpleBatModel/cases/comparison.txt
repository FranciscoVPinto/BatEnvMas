Comparison of experimental cases (8 total)

Common setup:
- dt_hours = 1, horizon = 500
- Loads use the same file data/load_cons.csv (A and B identical in these templates)
- PV uses data/pv_gen.csv (either per-house or shared pv_total)
- Battery MILP is unchanged; shared PV is implemented via preprocessing PV_i,t = alpha_i,t * PV_total,t

CASE 01 — 1 house baseline
- Single house "1" with moderate battery (E_max=5 kWh, P_ch=P_dis=2 kW)
- PV is directly available to the house (data.pv)
Expected: baseline cost and import/export patterns.

CASE 02 — 1 house bigger battery
- Same as CASE 01 but larger energy and power (E_max=12 kWh, P_ch=P_dis=4 kW)
Expected: more PV shifting, lower grid imports during expensive periods (if prices vary), higher self-consumption.

CASE 03 — 2 houses baseline (per-house PV)
- Houses A and B solved independently, each with PV (same pv file)
- A has medium battery; B has smaller battery
Expected: A more flexible than B; total behavior is basically the sum of two independent single-house problems.

CASE 04 — 2 houses baseline, bigger battery for B
- Same as CASE 03 but B battery is much larger (E_max=8 kWh, P_ch=P_dis=3 kW)
Expected: B imports less and exports less; higher PV utilization at B.

CASE 05 — 2 houses shared PV, equal alpha (50/50)
- Shared PV_total and fixed coefficients alpha_A=0.5, alpha_B=0.5
- Each house receives a fixed share of PV at each time step
Expected: compared to CASE 03, PV may be "misallocated" relative to instantaneous needs (fixed share), increasing export for one house while the other imports.

CASE 06 — 2 houses shared PV, weighted alpha (70/30)
- Shared PV_total with alpha_A=0.7, alpha_B=0.3
Expected: A benefits more from PV share; B tends to import more (unless battery differences compensate).

CASE 07 — 2 houses shared PV, unnormalized alphas + normalize=true
- Provided alphas (2, 1) are normalized to (2/3, 1/3) automatically
Expected: same qualitative behavior as a 66.7/33.3 share but tests the normalization logic and metadata warnings.

CASE 08 — 2 houses shared PV equal alpha, high buy price / low sell price
- Same as CASE 05 but tariffs grid_buy=0.40, grid_sell=0.03
Expected: stronger incentive to maximize self-consumption and battery usage; exports become less attractive.

Outputs:
- Each case produces results/<case_name>/results_house_<id>.csv and meta.yaml
- Plots are generated later by make_plots_all.py using plotset.yaml:
  - Per-case plots saved under results/<case_name>/plots/
  - Cross-case comparisons saved under results/_comparisons/<plotset_name>/
